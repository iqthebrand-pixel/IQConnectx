One agency, two clients in the same deal.

Brands and creators want different things from a sponsorship. We're paid by both to make sure the deal actually works for each of them.

For brands

Reach viewers who watch a review before they buy — not people scrolling past a pre-roll ad. We place your product with creators whose audience already trusts their opinion on hardware.

For creators

We find the sponsor, negotiate the rate, and manage the back-and-forth, so the deal doesn't cost you an afternoon of editing time. Creative control stays with you.

Every partnership sits in one lane: PC hardware, laptops, peripherals, and the software that runs them. We don't take on beauty, fitness, or lifestyle sponsorships — the whole point is knowing this category better than a generalist agency can.
