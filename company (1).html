A small agency built around one thing: technology creators.

IQ Connectx exists because tech sponsorships are a different job from every other influencer category — the audience is technical, the products are complicated, and a bad fit is obvious within the first thirty seconds of a video. We only work this lane.

How it started

Sponsorship outreach for hardware reviewers used to run through the same generalist agencies handling beauty, fitness, and gaming lifestyle talent — none of whom could tell a PCIe slot from a display port, or explain why that difference mattered to an audience of buyers.

IQ Connectx started by representing a handful of hardware review channels directly, negotiating rates and vetting sponsors one relationship at a time. The roster grew mostly by referral, as creators told other creators that deals were closing faster and sponsors were actually a fit for the channel.

That's still how the agency runs today: a small team, a narrow focus, and no interest in growing past what one team can vet properly.

What we optimize for

Fit over reach

We turn down partnerships where the product doesn't match the channel, even when the number attached to the deal is good. A bad-fit sponsorship costs a creator more in trust than it pays.

Speed for creators

Contracts move in days, not the six-to-eight-week cycle most agencies run on. Every week a deal sits in review is a week of content the creator isn't paid to make.

One category, on purpose

We don't split attention across beauty, gaming lifestyle, or general tech. PC hardware, laptops, and peripherals are the whole business, which is why we can tell a strong sponsor fit from a weak one quickly.

Since 2021

The roster's grown steadily rather than quickly — each stage below is a fair description of where the network stood at the time.

2021

Began representing the first three hardware review channels directly, handling sponsor outreach and rate negotiation.

2022

Crossed 250,000 combined subscribers across the roster; added a dedicated contract review step to every deal.

2024

Expanded from PC hardware into laptops and peripherals, following where the roster's own content was already heading.

2026

Network passes 560,000 subscribers and 118M+ average monthly views, with audience weighted across the US and India.
